# EI Mapper: Endurance Edition 0.2.1-alpha.32

## Camera and autosave controls

- Add a persistent viewport camera-speed control from 0.1x to 10x.
- Add Ctrl+mouse-wheel adjustment over the viewport; the modifier is
  configurable under **Settings > Key Bindings**.
- Show a non-activating 15-second notice before each scheduled recovery
  autosave. **Cancel Autosave** skips only that occurrence.
- Keep the notice visible as **Autosaving...** while the recovery files are
  actually being written; cancellation is disabled after writing begins.
- Manual **Autosave Now** remains immediate.
- Label the viewport control explicitly as **Camera Speed**.

## Selection responsiveness

- Selection-only undo/redo steps no longer rebuild the complete lossless MOB
  candidate or request family validation.
- Actual document changes continue to mark their MOB dirty and request the
  existing debounced family validation.
- Keep every selected object's row highlighted in the outliner, including
  viewport and direct Shift/Ctrl multi-selection.
- Restore the sticky selected-object hierarchy as soon as any ancestor row has
  scrolled outside the visible outliner area.
- Show a total object count instead of an arbitrary breadcrumb for
  multi-selection; single-object breadcrumbs are unchanged.
- Treat the Play from Here anchor as an exclusive selection target. Its sticky
  entry shows live XYZ coordinates, object selection clears it, and clearing
  the entrance removes the entry. Its clickable area is a projected capsule
  covering the complete hero outline and ground circle at the current camera
  angle.
- Keep the existing conservative Play from Here clearance check for this
  release. AI-graph-backed checks for valid standable meshes and platforms are
  tracked for 0.4.x instead of being approximated with unsafe heuristics.
